
The system is built using Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to ensure security and prevent unauthorized expenditure of API credits (like Alchemy RPC calls).

Here is the exact breakdown of the authority given to the three tiers:

### 1. ANALYST (Read-Only)
*   **Powers:** Can log in and view the dashboard, browse historical graphs, timelines, and read existing case files and data that is already in the Neo4j database. 
*   **Restrictions:** Cannot ingest new complaints, cannot trigger live on-chain traces (because traces consume API credits), and cannot manage users.
*   **Purpose:** Perfect for junior staff, auditors, or external agency observers who just need to read the data without modifying cases or spending system resources.

### 2. INVESTIGATOR (Standard Operative)
*   **Powers:** Has all Analyst privileges, **plus** the ability to actively ingest new complaints from the public, trigger live on-chain blockchain tracing, and use the AI Copilot to generate automated case reports.
*   **Restrictions:** Cannot access the Admin/User Management dashboard. Cannot use the highly resource-intensive Cross-Chain Correlation Engine.
*   **Purpose:** The standard role for your daily cyber crime operatives actively working on active cases.

### 3. SUPERVISOR (Admin)
*   **Powers:** The god-tier account. Has all Investigator privileges, **plus**:
    *   Full access to the **Admin Dashboard** to invite, deactivate, and permanently delete staff accounts.
    *   Exclusive access to the **Correlation Engine**, because correlating multi-hop paths between two distant wallets is incredibly computationally heavy and requires high-level clearance.
    *   *(Coming up next)*: Exclusive access to the **Audit Logs** to monitor every single action, login, and trace initiated by the staff below them.
*   **Purpose:** Reserved for Cyber Cell chiefs and system administrators (like your `admin@cybercell.gov.in` account).
